About

Research depth meets
production engineering

I work on the standards an AI system must meet before it reaches frontline work at population scale — where fairness, evidence, and accountability stop being research questions and become deployment constraints.

My published work reduced demographic bias by 30.8% in clinical models with no loss of performance, and cut cross-modal disparity from 7.6% to 4.5% in multimodal diagnosis. At BRAC I apply the same standard to deployment — shaping policy on responsible AI use, data protection for AI products, and the evidence a system must produce before it reaches people. The research and the governance work are the same problem approached from two ends.

Growing up in rural Bangladesh, where healthcare access was limited, I saw at first hand the need to democratise medical care and education. That experience led me to found OgroPath, an AI platform making medical entrance-exam preparation accessible to students nationwide.
